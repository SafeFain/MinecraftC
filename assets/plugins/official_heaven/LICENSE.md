# Official Heaven resources

Starstep geometry and palette are original MinecraftC assets, exported from the
existing five-box model and deterministic held-tool palette by `tools/heaven_model.py`.
They retain the project's license. No external artwork is included.

The Heaven material frames, normal/property maps and inventory icons under
`textures/` are original procedural MinecraftC artwork under CC0-1.0, as documented
in `assets/textures/LICENSE.md`. Their source definitions live in this package;
`tools/texture_generator.py` and the texture workbench export their pixels through
the shared authoring atlas. Migration retains the original pixels and logical slots.

`audio/heaven_ether.wav` and `audio/heaven_sanctum.wav` are original deterministic
MinecraftC compositions generated by `tools/audio_generator.py`, under GPL-3.0-only.
Generator revision 2 develops the original themes with new voicings, instruments
and periodic stereo effects. The regenerated assets retain stereo 16-bit PCM,
24 kHz encoding and complete loops (96 beats each).

The ten language dictionaries were moved from the existing project translations
and retain the repository's license. `resources.sha256.json` records the complete
package resource inventory used by installation/archive checks on all platforms.
