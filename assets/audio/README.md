# MinecraftC Music

These eight original MinecraftC compositions are generated by the standard-library
Python renderer `tools/audio_generator.py`, with themes and arrangements in
`tools/audio_score.py`. They are project assets under GPL-3.0-only; no samples,
soundfonts or external compositions are used. Generator revision 2 revoices and
develops the original themes into introductions, contrasting phrases and varied
returns instead of lengthening them by repeating an unchanged loop.

| File | Arrangement | BPM | Beats | Seconds |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| `menu_whimsy.wav` | Bell theme, soft plucks and answering piano | 104 | 96 | 55.38 |
| `menu_spark.wav` | Crystalline ornaments and alternating arpeggio density | 112 | 96 | 51.43 |
| `gameplay_calm.wav` | Warm piano, sustained strings and gently moving harmony | 64 | 128 | 120.00 |
| `overworld_solitude.wav` | Close felt-style piano with space between melodic phrases | 60 | 96 | 96.00 |
| `overworld_horizon.wav` | Low bowed harmonies and distant melodic calls | 72 | 128 | 106.67 |
| `overworld_nightfall.wav` | Dark pads and isolated glass notes | 54 | 96 | 106.67 |
| `../plugins/official_heaven/audio/heaven_ether.wav` | Breathing pads, floating bells and high responses | 56 | 96 | 102.86 |
| `../plugins/official_heaven/audio/heaven_sanctum.wav` | Soft organ chorale, connected voices and distant chimes | 48 | 96 | 120.00 |

The menu independently chooses between its two tracks, the Overworld between its
four tracks, and the official Heaven plugin between its two tracks on entry and
after each complete track. Playback selection, volume controls and transitions
are unchanged by the arrangement revision.

## Rendering

Run from the repository root; only Python 3.10+ and its standard library are needed:

```bash
python3 tools/audio_generator.py
python3 tools/audio_generator.py --list
python3 tools/audio_generator.py --track menu_whimsy --track menu_spark
python3 tools/audio_generator.py --output build-local/music/audio --report build-local/music/report.json
python3 tools/audio_generator.py --track heaven_ether --heaven-output build-local/heaven/audio
```

Without filters, all eight tracks are rendered. Repeated `--track` arguments select
a subset in album order; duplicate names render once and unknown names are errors.
`--output` defaults to `assets/audio`. Unless `--heaven-output` is explicit, Heaven
tracks go to `plugins/official_heaven/audio` beside that output directory. Rendering
Heaven music refreshes an existing package `resources.sha256.json` after generation;
rendering only menu/Overworld tracks leaves that inventory alone. `--list` writes
no files. `--report` writes JSON with generator version, rate and each track's
duration, frames, BPM, beats, peak/dBFS, RMS, per-channel DC offset, stereo difference
RMS, loop seam and WAV SHA-256. Metrics describe final quantized PCM; RMS is not an
integrated perceptual loudness/LUFS measurement.

The synthesis bank contains piano, bell, pluck, bowed string, pad, organ and air
voices. Band-limited harmonic wave tables, separate transient decays, envelopes,
detuning and slow amplitude movement differentiate them. A bounded note cache
avoids resynthesizing identical notes. Per-track integer hashes provide repeatable
timing, velocity and pan variations without global random state. Bass placement
is centered, with equal-power panning above the low register.

Overlapping notes wrap into the next cycle. Four damped feedback delays are warmed
with periodic source audio before publication, and stereo beat echoes also wrap.
Mastering removes per-channel DC and applies one constant gain toward the track's
RMS target, capped at 0.88 peak (below -1 dBFS); it preserves phrase dynamics without
hard clipping or a whole-track fade to silence at every loop boundary.

All assets remain stereo 16-bit little-endian PCM WAV at 24 kHz, in complete
24/32-bar loops. Their combined PCM payload is approximately 69.5 MiB. Existing
SDL decoding and plugin budgets support them without a runtime format change.

## Verification

```bash
python3 tests/test_audio_assets.py
python3 tests/test_official_package.py
```

The music test covers score/seed variation, seven voices, envelope endpoints,
out-of-band partial removal, cache independence, centered bass, wrapping notes,
periodic reverb, DC removal, mastering, CLI routing and reports. It checks the
committed hashes and completely regenerates all eight WAVs for byte comparison,
including effects and mastering. This takes longer than the earlier prefix-only
test. Format, duration, RMS, peak, stereo difference and loop seams are checked
independently. Listen to phrase transitions and two consecutive cycles as well;
numeric checks do not establish subjective musical quality.
